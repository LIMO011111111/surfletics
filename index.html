Clickable prototype of the Surfletics app

Sign up, then see every forecast scored for your level and board. All schools, prices and forecasts are examples, and everything you enter stays in this browser.

  2. Switch boards on the Spots tab and watch the fit scores change
  3. Open a spot and drag along the day curve
  4. Book a lesson, then rate it – your sweet spot learns
